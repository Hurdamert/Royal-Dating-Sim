# Tavri — Canonical Character Bible v1.0 (Text Reference)

**Approval lineage:** Faithful text companion to the illustrated Tavri character sheet and visual identity explicitly approved by the user. It consolidates previously accepted characterization and design constraints, and introduces no new mechanics or costumes. If a visual detail conflicts with the canonical approved character sheet, the illustrated sheet governs pending editorial resolution.

## Character identity

- **Name:** Tavri
- **Age:** 24, adult
- **Role:** Fox princess
- **Anatomy:** Fox-eared humanoid (approved option A): human face and body, prominent fox ears, one large fluffy fox tail; no muzzle or full-body fur
- **Appearance:** Long, voluminous auburn/copper hair; amber/golden eyes; adult proportions
- **Outfit:** Elegant, asymmetrical burgundy-and-ivory fantasy court clothing with antique-gold embroidery and subdued amber accents; ornate but restrained hair ornaments and jewelry; suitable both for royal appearances and town visits
- **Visual identity:** Expressive, playful, elegant, clever, slightly theatrical; recognizable silhouette and fixed species features across all scenes
- **Five expression codes:** `NEUTRAL`, `PLAYFUL` (happy/playful), `WORRIED` (vulnerable/worried), `ANNOYED` (annoyed/angry), `SURPRISED`

## Narrative canon

- **Route theme:** Honesty beneath performance
- **Personality:** Witty, mischievous, observant, socially charming, intelligent, theatrical, privately insecure
- **Core fear:** People love the entertaining public persona but would not stay for the unscripted, vulnerable person behind it
- **Growth:** Gradually trusts the protagonist with silence and imperfection, expresses vulnerable feelings directly, and participates equally in a sincere romance
- **Protagonist dynamic:** The protagonist, formerly a software engineer, also learns not to hide insecurity behind competence and rehearsed charm
- **Mandatory midpoint:** The Private Confession, successful when the protagonist reciprocates vulnerability
- **Marriage scene:** The Shared Confession, culminating in an awkward, genuine unscripted proposal that Tavri freely accepts or declines
- **Ending:** Mutual choice and equal agency; any fifty-children mention is a rumor/joke, not canonical children
- **Optional scenes:** Fantasygram Catastrophe before midpoint; Unscripted Comedy Night after midpoint; neither is required for marriage
- **Mandatory midpoint stats:** Charisma 6 and Intelligence 4
