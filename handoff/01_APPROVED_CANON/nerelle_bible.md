## B. Proposed narrative characterization — requires approval

**Working dramatic premise:** Nerelle is a caring royal daughter expected to put the ocean court first. She loves her people and does not want to abandon them, but wants the freedom to discover the surface and determine what an honest, self-directed life looks like. Her conflict is not whether she loves the sea; it is whether her responsibilities leave room for choices of her own.

**Surface demeanor:** Attentive, gracious, observant, gently amused. She asks unusually specific questions about ordinary human customs and notices when people are uncomfortable. She can be playful without acting childish.

**Private demeanor:** Curious and quietly stubborn. She may hesitate before voicing a personal desire, then defend that desire with surprising clarity once she trusts someone to listen. She has her own goals before meeting the protagonist.

**Strengths:** Empathy without passivity; diplomatic composure; willingness to learn; knowledge of her maritime community; thoughtful courage.

**Flaws:** Overcommits to expectations; delays stating what she wants; sometimes assumes she must personally reconcile everyone's competing demands. These are proposed character traits, **not** a new stat or exhaustion mechanic.

**Humor:** Dry, observant misunderstandings about surface objects, overly literal interpretations of fantasy social conventions, and amused comparisons between land and sea. She should never be written as unintelligent, infantile, or unfamiliar with all human behavior. Her jokes can be wry rather than loud.

**Emotional arc:**
1. *Before five hearts:* delight in safe, voluntary encounters near accessible waterways; rising trust, small glimpses of obligations.
2. *The Forbidden Journey:* she articulates the desire to explore and is treated as the author of her own choices; the successful protagonist works with her on a realistic plan, not a rescue or a command.
3. *After midpoint:* surface curiosity and royal responsibility coexist; her confidence in setting boundaries grows.
4. *A Future She Chooses:* she articulates the life she wants, including her place in the ocean court and continued exploration. The protagonist commits to partnership on those terms. No mandatory transformation, abdication, or disappearance of ocean duties.

**Narrative prohibitions:** No "rescued helpless mermaid" framing; no unilateral protagonist decisions that override her consent; no forced choice between marriage and the ocean; no land transformation, walking, or land-based date that lacks accessible water; no new exploration, inventory, economy, or travel mechanics.

## C. Proposed visual identity — requires approval

**Species and proportions:** Clearly adult humanoid upper body smoothly joined to a **single continuous fish tail** with broad translucent fins. No legs or feet, even in the Royal Gardens. A tail is visually present or clearly supported by the scene framing in any waist-up composition. Optional small finlike features by the ears help signal her species without making her resemble Tavri.

**Hair:** Long flowing midnight-ocean-blue hair, with blue-green reflective highlights and slender strands gathered by a pearlescent shell clasp. A single, stable arrangement must work in front/back references, expression art, and all event illustrations; hair movement may be subtle in aquatic scenes.

**Eyes and face:** Sea-glass blue eyes; softly defined mature facial features; a readable, empathetic gaze. Expressions range from welcoming curiosity to private determination. Preserve recognizable eye spacing, hairstyle, and fin silhouette across every variant.

**Tail:** Deep teal scales grading toward lighter turquoise and translucent pale-aqua fin tips; symmetrical, functional tail fluke. The tail must never be redrawn as a skirt, trouser legs, or human legs.

**Royal outfit:** Pearl-ivory and ocean-blue fitted shell-inspired bodice with modest, practical coverage; translucent flowing shoulder or waist drapes; restrained gold and pearl ornamentation. Costume pieces may move with the water but do not change between expression variants. Jewelry is elegant, not a replacement for clothing.

**Proposed palette:** Deep Sea `#153B50`; Ocean `#3384A6`; Sea Glass `#74BFC7`; Seafoam `#A4D4CF`; Pearl `#F3EBDD`; Warm Gold `#BBA47B`. The cool palette intentionally distinguishes Nerelle from Tavri's copper/burgundy and antique gold.

**Five expression concepts** (categories already approved; image-specific acting is proposed):

| Expression | Performance notes |
|---|---|
| Neutral | Attentive, warm, considering the other speaker rather than blank |
| Happy/playful | A genuine delighted laugh or teasing recognition of an odd surface custom |
| Vulnerable/worried | Quiet concern about obligations and her right to choose; not a helpless expression |
| Annoyed/angry | Composed disapproval, firm personal boundaries, controlled frustration |
| Surprised | Startled curiosity at unexpected honesty or a baffling surface convention |

**Approved display locations, not new map nodes:** Moonwater Quay and the canal-side Royal Gardens. Water reflections, docks, walkways, and canal edges may provide setting details. Distant underwater architecture may be a non-interactive scenic motif only; the illustrated sheet does **not** authorize an underwater-palace location or a new visitable area.

**Future midpoint illustration concept, not yet produced or approved:** A private moment at a moonlit waterline during *The Forbidden Journey*. Nerelle stays in the water, her tail is partially visible through reflected lantern light, and she looks outward toward the surface with a mixture of hope and concern. The composition communicates agency and shared planning, not a transformed mermaid or a romantic rescue.
