## B. Proposed characterization — editorial approval needed

**Dramatic premise.** Kaedra has spent years being treated as a shield for the court and a frightening spectacle to strangers. She is genuinely capable and accepts her protective role, but dislikes the assumption that strength eliminates her need for gentleness, uncertainty, or affection. Her story is about allowing herself to be known without surrendering power or dignity.

**Public manner.** Quietly commanding, direct, precise and difficult to read. Rather than boasting, she makes small observations that reveal considerable knowledge of safety, procedure and people. Ordinary courtiers interpret her silences as anger; often she is simply deciding whether a joke would land.

**Private manner.** Soft-spoken, surprisingly attentive, and fond of modest affectionate gestures. Her humor is dry, blunt and occasionally accidental. When she relaxes, she can laugh at the absurdity of how much ceremony people put around her. Her gentleness is not a magical secret personality that appears suddenly: quiet care is observable before the midpoint if someone pays attention.

**Strengths.** Competence under pressure; willingness to protect bystanders; diligence; loyalty without demands for worship; ability to own her decisions and apologize when appropriate. She should retain agency and personal responsibility at every stage.

**Flaws.** Expects others to be afraid of her; tends to stand apart rather than risk rejection; sometimes equates explaining anxiety with burdening others; may make unilateral protective decisions out of habit. These are *character traits*, not new combat, fear, reputation or relationship mechanics.

**Humor rules.** Favor deadpan banter, formal royal absurdity, awkward well-meant affection, the prince's overly elaborate fashion contest, and rumors that misread her behavior. Never make her a violent caricature or turn the romance into a campaign to tame or cure her. Humpty can provide comic observations without becoming an additional paid action or quest.

**Emotional arc.** Before five hearts, the protagonist sees how others react to her presence and begins noticing acts of care. At **The Royal Trial**, an unexpected small magic flare makes her fear others will only remember her danger; the correct protagonist response secures everyone and approaches her privately with empathy, not a public display or a battle. Afterward, she practices voicing her needs while remaining a capable guardian. During **Private Vow**, she chooses a partner who sees strength and tenderness together and does not treat her as an object of devotion or a project to repair.

**Narrative boundaries.** Her magic flare is a scripted narrative event, not unlockable combat, an independent magic meter, a dangerous uncontrolled catastrophe, or a new training activity. Characters may overreact comically, but the event must remain small enough to be resolved by safety precautions and honest dialogue. Kaedra never loses her physical strength to become lovable. No canon-required shapeshifting, new dragon form, flight ability, equipment progression, wealth system, or additional map location is introduced by this candidate.

## C. Proposed visual identity — **not approved canon**

**Character anatomy:** Primarily humanoid adult woman with clearly draconic markers: **two curved dark bronze-black horns**, small matte scales at shoulders and forearms, **one powerful dark-crimson ridged dragon tail**, and **folded leathery dragon wings** partially visible behind her in the illustrated concept. Her face and hands remain primarily human. Wings are proposed visual anatomy **only**; the illustration does not authorize flight gameplay, a dragon-form transformation, combat, or additional traversal mechanics. The user may instead choose the earlier explored wingless silhouette.

**Apparent age and build:** Clearly adult, approximately 26. Taller-seeming, broad-shouldered, athletic and composed; imposing posture but expressive, approachable facial proportions once her guard drops. Avoid exaggerated monstrous anatomy and childlike features.

**Hair:** Long **deep crimson / dark wine-red** waves with darker aubergine shadows, braided back from the horns. This illustrated alternative differs from the previously explored ash-silver proposal (still available as an *unapproved alternate*). It must be a deliberately darker, more saturated red than Tavri's brighter copper-auburn so that the princesses remain recognizable at a glance.

**Eyes:** **Amber-gold** with a deep rust outer ring, slightly brighter than the costume's bronze accents. Their shape and her more angular brows should distinguish her from Tavri's amber eyes; eye color has no magic-meter or gameplay meaning.

**Horns, wings and tail:** Paired matte black-bronze horns curl outward and upward from the crown. A **single deep-crimson tail** with armored scale ridges tapers behind the gown, unlike Tavri's fluffy tail or Nerelle's fluke. Paired membrane wings fold behind the shoulders when visible. Keep the number, attachment points, and relative horn/tail/wing sizes consistent across future art.

**Clothing:** Structured **black, garnet-red and antique-bronze court armor/gown** with a high-standing collar and asymmetrical royal panels, articulated bronze shoulder detailing, fitted dark bodice, and restrained draconic motifs. It reads as formal guardian regalia, **not equippable armor or a gear system**. The candidate artwork contains a dramatic neckline; approval would establish that visual silhouette unless the reviewer requests more coverage.

**Proposed illustrated palette (not final):** Charcoal `#242025`, Oxblood `#772B2F`, Garnet Hair `#922F35`, Antique Bronze `#AD7952`, Ember Gold `#D6A46F`, Parchment `#E8D5BC`. Compared with Tavri's copper hair and ivory outfit, Kaedra uses darker, more armored silhouettes and dramatically curled horns; Nerelle retains her distinct aquatic blues.

**Silhouette guidance:** A firm vertical pose, high structured collar, curved paired horns, dark-crimson hair, folded wings and armored tail form her readable outline. Tail and wings must be anatomically coherent in side/back views. She can soften her posture visibly without changing clothes or species.

### Five eventual expression categories (concept studies, not final sprite approvals)

| Expression | Proposed visual performance |
|---|---|
| Neutral | Steady gaze, poised jaw, quiet protective attention—not permanently scowling |
| Happy/playful | Rare but unforced small smile; eyes relax, dry amusement shows |
| Vulnerable/worried | Controlled composure loosens; glance lowered, slight tension in brows; tenderness and uncertainty without helplessness |
| Annoyed/angry | Firm stare and narrowed brows; displeasure stays contained and never implies immediate attack |
| Surprised | Brief loss of composure, widened eyes and subtly raised brows, including being startled by an unexpectedly sincere compliment |

### Midpoint illustration concept — future approval only

**The Royal Trial:** In the Palace Court, a small unexpected magical flare briefly interrupts a formal demonstration. Bystanders remain safe or are safely guided back, and Kaedra stands visibly unsettled by their fear. A sheltered arch or private corner can foreshadow the later comfort scene. No fight, catastrophe, fully transformed dragon or additional venue may appear. Important emotions must also be conveyed through dialogue and accessible text.

## D. Art-production and narrative constraints

1. The associated candidate sheet is exploratory **character concept art**, not any of the five final production sprites and not the final Royal Trial illustration.
2. Keep the v1.0 Tavri and Nerelle references frozen. Kaedra must be identifiable by horns, dark formal armor-gown and angular body language even if the final hair color remains in the red family.
3. If this sheet is approved, produce exactly five expression sprites with coherent costume design and consistently placed horns, eyes, scales and tail; produce the dedicated midpoint illustration only afterward.
4. Keep dialogue and emotional cues accessible via text. No critical information may be communicated by wing pose, horn glow, colored magic or facial expressions alone.
5. Any generated lettering inside concept art is illustrative and not authoritative. The written bible and approved decision register control name, age, stats and event titles.
6. Treat the shared prologue and failure ending as provisional and **excluded** from the character-specific freeze, as in Tavri's and Nerelle's approved references.
7. **No Kaedra pivotal scripts, production sprites, or game implementation are approved or delivered here.**
